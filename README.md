# Cybersecurity GRC Toolkit

A practical, customizable Excel workbook designed to support cybersecurity Governance, Risk, and Compliance activities and hands-on learning.

## Features

31 worksheets covering:
- Framework references and regulatory obligations
- Control libraries, assessments, and evidence tracking
- Inherent and residual risk scoring, treatment plans, and risk history
- Audit findings, corrective actions, and overdue tracking
- Compliance exceptions and expiry alerts
- Third-party assessments and privacy governance
- Policy reviews, incidents, and business continuity
- KPI definitions and results
- An executive dashboard and 12 charts covering compliance, risks, and operations
- Control mapping, regulatory review schedules, change logs, and QA records
- Dropdown lists, conditional formatting, and navigation links

## Reference Library

Includes references to NCA ECC, CSCC, CCC, SAMA CSF, PDPL, ISO/IEC 27001, NIST CSF, and CIS Controls.

## Download and Use

1. Open the `.xlsx` file listed in this repository.
2. Select **Download raw file** to download the workbook.
3. Open it in Microsoft Excel.
4. Start with the **Index** and **Methodology** worksheets.
5. Adapt the registers, assessments, and tracking fields to your needs.

## Scope

This is an independent practical resource for learning and organizing GRC activities. Adapt it to your organization’s requirements and verify references against official sources.

Use of this workbook does not establish compliance or certification.
